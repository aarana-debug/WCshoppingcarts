Cart Containment Wheel Systems

We're a certified vendor and repair specialist for the three most widely used cart containment systems in Southern California — Gatekeeper, Rocateq, and Carttronics.

What are cart containment wheels?

Cart containment systems use specially designed electronic wheels that lock automatically when a shopping cart is pushed beyond a designated perimeter — typically the edge of a parking lot. When triggered by a buried wire or radio frequency signal, the wheel locks in place, stopping the cart and protecting your inventory. West Coast Shopping Cart Service installs, services, and repairs all three leading systems.

Cart Containment System
Gatekeeper Systems
Intellilock · Perimeter Plus

Gatekeeper is the industry standard for electronic cart containment, protecting millions of carts at retailers across North America. Their Intellilock wheels lock automatically when a cart approaches a buried perimeter wire at the lot's edge — no staff required. Once locked, the cart cannot be pushed further off-property, and it automatically resets when returned inside the perimeter.

  • Triggered by buried perimeter wire — fully automatic
  • Self-resetting — no staff intervention needed
  • Compatible with standard 5" caster mount
  • WCSC installs, replaces, and repairs Gatekeeper wheels on-site
Cart Containment System
Rocateq
RF-Based Perimeter Control

Rocateq uses radio frequency technology to define a virtual perimeter around your property. Their distinctive red-capped caster locks the cart wheel the moment it crosses the RF boundary — without any buried wire infrastructure. This makes Rocateq particularly popular for retrofitting existing lots and locations where trenching isn't practical.

  • RF-based — no buried wire, minimal installation disruption
  • Visual red indicator makes locked carts easy to identify
  • Ideal for retrofits and multi-building retail centers
  • WCSC services and replaces Rocateq casters across Southern California
Cart Containment System
Carttronics
CAPS Caster System

Carttronics' CAPS (Cart Access & Payment System) casters bring containment and access control together in one rugged unit. Common at Costco, Aldi, and select Walmart locations, CAPS casters can be paired with coin-deposit mechanisms or standalone containment perimeters. The heavy-duty chrome yoke and reinforced housing make them one of the most durable systems on the market.

  • Heavy-duty CAPS caster — built for high-volume environments
  • Compatible with coin-deposit and perimeter lock systems
  • Used by Costco, Aldi, and major retail chains
  • WCSC stocks and replaces Carttronics CAPS casters on-site
