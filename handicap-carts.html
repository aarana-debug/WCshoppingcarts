What are motorized shopping carts?

Motorized or power shopping carts give customers with mobility limitations the ability to shop independently. These electric vehicles combine a standard shopping basket with a seated, self-propelled scooter base. West Coast Shopping Cart Service is equipped to service, repair, and retrieve the three most common brands found in Southern California retail stores — Mart, Amigo, and Dane Technologies SmartKART.

Motorized Mobility Cart
Mart Cart
Electric Power Shopping Cart

The Mart Cart is one of the most widely used motorized shopping carts in North America and a staple at major retailers like Walmart and Sam's Club. Built with a sturdy black steel frame, padded seat, and full-size wire shopping basket, the Mart Cart is designed for heavy daily use in high-traffic retail environments. West Coast Shopping Cart Service can service, repair, and retrieve Mart Carts across our entire Southern California coverage area.

  • Common at Walmart, Sam's Club, and large-format retail
  • Battery service, charging system checks, and motor repair
  • Basket, seat, and frame repairs handled on-site
  • Retrieval from off-site locations included in service routes
Motorized Mobility Cart
Amigo
The Original Power Shopping Cart

Amigo Mobility International pioneered the powered shopping cart and has been the industry standard for decades. Recognizable by its green Amigo logo and distinctive tiller-style steering column, the Amigo cart is built for reliability and ease of use. It's a common sight at grocery chains, pharmacies, and home improvement stores throughout Southern California. We service all Amigo models and carry common replacement parts.

  • Found at grocery chains, pharmacies, and home improvement stores
  • Tiller steering, seat, and basket repair and replacement
  • Battery and electrical system diagnostics
  • Full service history documentation provided upon request

Dane Technologies
SmartKART Power Shopping Cart

Dane Technologies' SmartKART stands out with its bold red chassis and compact, maneuverable design. Built for tight store aisles and high-cycle retail environments, the SmartKART features a robust motor, oversized shopping basket, and ergonomic seating. It's popular at membership-based retailers and specialty grocery stores where durability and maneuverability are both critical. WCSC services and retrieves SmartKARTs throughout our Southern California territory.

  • Compact design built for tight aisles and high-cycle use
  • Motor, drive system, and battery pack service
  • Seat, frame, and basket repair handled on-site
  • Retrieval included with all active service accounts
